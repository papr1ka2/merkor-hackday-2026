## What triggered extra rounds
- Switching instruments: StudioCo had already accepted the evaluation agreement (Turn 2) when AuditCo moved to an MSA + SOW for paid work, which opened a second, much heavier negotiation.
- StudioCo's counsel markup (Turn 5) mixed acceptable clean-ups with one material risk — §1.2 negligence liability, inconsistent with the gross-negligence indemnity trigger in the same draft — which AuditCo escalated to its own counsel.
- AuditCo counsel's threshold question (is the product used in a manual test?) and later backlog left no counter-turn; work proceeded without closing the MSA, and the matter was dropped.
