## What triggered extra rounds
- Turn 1's prepaid, non-refundable structure with recovery-limited refunds → counsel rebuilt payment around escrow and direct refunds in turn 3.
- The vendor's quote expired on Oct 12, before counsel's turn → counsel conditioned funding on a renewed quote and a binding Outside Delivery Date.
- On the Nov 4 call AuditCo relayed counsel's points in summary and let HardwareCo take the next pen → HardwareCo's turn 5 crossed counsel's turn 3, so at least one more round is needed to reconcile the two drafts.
- Pre-filled compliance exhibits in turn 1 → counsel flagged that AuditCo must verify each answer before signing, an open item on AuditCo's side.
