## What triggered extra rounds
- The GC's competitive-products carve-out in "Derivative Works" (turn 2) set off three more turns: counsel deleted it (turn 3), ChainCo partly rejected that (turn 4), and AuditCo narrowed it (turn 5).
- Counsel's neutral rewrite resolved AuditCo's concern but not ChainCo's competing-product concern, which is why the issue reopened.
- The leftover reference to a previous customer in AuditCo's template had to be corrected by the counterparty.
- A substantive change (§3.3 termination for convenience) entered at signing, after the last redline turn.
