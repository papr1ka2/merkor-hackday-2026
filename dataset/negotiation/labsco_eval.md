## What triggered extra rounds
- LabsCo's own MSA (turn 0) was a paid-services contract; replacing it with AuditCo's evaluation form (turn 1) restarted the negotiation from AuditCo's paper.
- LabsCo's turn 2 added security covenants and a release carve-out → AuditCo counsel's turn 3 removed or softened them → LabsCo accepted in turn 4 but compensated with a wider Confidential Information definition and limits on AuditCo's use and disclosure of results.
- Several of LabsCo's turn-4 edits and the final one-month Evaluation Period were made without tracked changes, so the only reliable record of what changed is a comparison of the full texts.
